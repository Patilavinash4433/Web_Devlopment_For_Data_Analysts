Type of developers

There are two types of developers in the software world - good developers and bad developers. Let's explore the key differences between these two categories.

✅ Good developer

A good developer behaves nicely with their team members, follows best practices, writes clean and maintainable code, and continuously learns new technologies.

❌ Bad developer

A bad developer behaves poorly towards the team, ignores best practices, writes messy code, and refuses to improve their skills.
